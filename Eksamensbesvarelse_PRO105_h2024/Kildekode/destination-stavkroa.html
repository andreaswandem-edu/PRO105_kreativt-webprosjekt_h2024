Stavkroa

If you are looking to have a bit more fun on your trip to Norway, Stavkroa is the place to go.

Stavkroa is an iconic after-ski destination in Hemsedal, known for its unforgettable parties. It is located right by the slopes, so it's the perfect way to end a day of skiing.
