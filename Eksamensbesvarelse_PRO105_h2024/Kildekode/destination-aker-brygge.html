Aker Brygge

Aker brygge might be one of the most popular waterfront areas in Oslo. Here you'll find a cozy atmopshere, plenty of shopping options and dining.

Enjoy a walk down by the waterfront, or go on a wild shopping spree, the choice is yours. Anywyas, Aker brygge is a must-visit destinaton.
